## 1. Purpose and status of this record

This document records the development and intellectual-property position supplied by the project owner and the copyright notices currently present in this repository. It is an internal project record intended to preserve provenance, clarify the distinction between project materials and third-party components, and support later legal, technical, and release reviews.

It is not a legal opinion, a government registration, a formal chain-of-title investigation, or independent verification of every contribution. It does not create or transfer rights. Any legal conclusion about ownership depends on the facts, applicable law, contributor agreements, employment or contractor arrangements (if any), and the relevant third-party licences.
