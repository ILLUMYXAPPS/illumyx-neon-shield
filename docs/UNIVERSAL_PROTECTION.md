# Universal Protection Profiles

Neon Shield uses one underlying match/evidence architecture with configurable protection profiles.

Users choose what they want to protect:

- Music & Audio
- Documents
- Images & Artwork
- Video
- Projects & Code
- Custom Files

A profile selects the evidence types appropriate to the protected material. It does not itself access remote devices, collect credentials, make legal determinations, or submit automated claims.

The ILLUMYX music workflow can use the Music & Audio profile without making music-specific assumptions part of the universal engine.
